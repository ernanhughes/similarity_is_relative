# Similarity Is Relative

RELATE is evidence-first research into whether frozen representations contain useful relational signals that their default similarity geometry fails to expose—and whether those signals can be recovered, verified, compared with strong alternatives and rejected when unsupported.

The central idea is simple:

> Similarity is not one property of an embedding. It is a relation defined by the question being asked.

A frozen representation may support several notions of similarity even when raw cosine or Euclidean distance exposes them poorly. RELATE investigates whether those relations can be recovered as independently testable evidence, executed as declared queries and refused when the required evidence is weak, absent, outside the declared model family or unstable under shift.

Claims begin as falsifiable questions, become executable contracts and are promoted only after committed evidence, explicit decision rules and appropriately classified verification.

## What RELATE has established

Option B tested repository-separated Python functions, frozen `microsoft/codebert-base` embeddings and three independently predicted AST-derived primitives:

- cyclomatic complexity;
- maximum control nesting depth;
- distinct call-site count.

The preregistered hard-negative result was:

```text
raw cosine:                 0.532458984375
raw Euclidean:              0.533314453125
predicted primitive model:  0.732851562500
best-raw gap:               0.199537109375
outcome:                    REAL_PREMISE_SUPPORTED
```

The complete `4,000 × 5` primary query-score matrix and final decision were independently recomputed by a separate implementation.

The promoted claim remains narrow:

> In repository-separated real Python code, independently predicted AST primitive coordinates exposed a frozen three-way structural relation materially better than raw CodeBERT cosine or Euclidean geometry on the preregistered hard-negative test.

This supports the premise that a real frozen representation can contain useful relational information that its default geometry materially underexposes.

## What RELATE has not established

RELATE has not yet demonstrated:

- a general relational-composition algorithm;
- semantic binding across arbitrary relation identities;
- calibrated recovery in a prospectively defined cosine-failure stratum;
- safe refusal for unsupported recovered scores;
- superiority to a directly trained calibrated model;
- production-quality nearest-neighbour retrieval;
- transfer across embedding models, programming languages or domains;
- conceptual retrieval in writing.

E01 remains closed because its weighted product-space results were valid positive controls but did not establish the original general-composition thesis. No later experiment inherits E01 success.

## Option E — cosine failure and relational recovery

Option E is the active scientific programme.

Its question is:

> When raw cosine similarity is uninformative for an externally verified relationship, can RELATE recover evidence of that relationship from the frozen representation, and can calibrated support distinguish genuine hidden signal from weak, absent, out-of-family or shifted signal more effectively than strong calibrated alternatives?

Option E separates three quantities:

```text
default cosine score
relation-specific recovered score
calibrated evidence supporting action on that score
```

A high recovered score with weak support is not a successful retrieval. It is a refusal case.

The core relationship to Hallucination Energy is:

> Hallucination Energy measures semantic content unsupported by attached evidence; RELATE Option E measures relational evidence supported by a frozen representation but underexposed by its default geometry.

This is a conceptual relationship, not a claim of mathematical equivalence.

### E-DISCOVERY

E-DISCOVERY may construct a prospectively defined cosine-failure stratum, compare candidate recovery and support mechanisms, test strong direct baselines and determine whether a non-degenerate confirmation is justified.

It must end with exactly one outcome:

```text
E_CONFIRMATION_JUSTIFIED
E_CONFIRMATION_NOT_JUSTIFIED
E_DATA_FIREWALL_FAILED
E_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED
```

E-DISCOVERY remains exploratory and cannot promote `E-RECOVER-001`.

### E-CONFIRM

E-CONFIRM may begin only after a documentation-only contract freezes:

- one external relation and label source;
- one frozen representation;
- the cosine-failure rule;
- recovery and support mechanisms;
- direct and modular calibrated baselines;
- matched-coverage evaluation;
- the primary material margin;
- final reserve selection;
- independent recomputation;
- permitted publication language.

E-CONFIRM runs once. A failed primary rule cannot be rescued by secondary metrics under the same identifier.

## Why code remains the first application

Code contains measurable relationships beyond textual resemblance:

- control-flow and data-flow structure;
- side-effect and exception behaviour;
- call-graph and dependency roles;
- algorithmic family;
- architectural responsibility;
- duplicated business rules;
- equivalent transformations expressed with different syntax and names.

That makes code a strong proving ground because target relations can be defined outside the embedding. The first serious application should deliberately include:

- true relations with low cosine;
- high-cosine surface false positives;
- easy positives;
- true negatives;
- weak, absent, out-of-family and shifted cases.

Option B established only a bounded structural premise. Conceptual duplicate retrieval and support-aware refusal remain future Option E experiments.

## Writing as a later application

Writing contains relationships that topic-level semantic similarity often collapses:

- the same concept expressed through different vocabulary;
- the same causal structure applied to different subjects;
- repeated arguments or contradictions;
- shared narrative function;
- thematic and metaphorical recurrence;
- the evolution of an idea through drafts and author decisions.

This is a proposed application, not an established RELATE result. A writing programme requires author-grounded labels, manuscript lineage, selected and rejected revisions, explicit concept definitions and human evaluation. Model-generated labels cannot serve as the sole ground truth.
