# Similarity Is Relative

RELATE is evidence-first research into whether frozen representations contain useful relational signals that their default similarity geometry fails to expose—and whether those signals can be queried, verified, compared with strong alternatives and rejected when unsupported.

The central idea is simple:

> Similarity is not one property of an embedding. It is a relation defined by the question being asked.

A frozen representation may support several different notions of similarity even when raw cosine or Euclidean distance exposes them poorly. RELATE investigates whether those relations can be recovered as explicit, independently testable coordinates, executed as declared queries and refused when the required evidence is weak, absent or shifted.

Claims begin as falsifiable questions, become executable contracts and are promoted only after committed evidence, explicit decision rules and appropriately classified verification.

## What RELATE has established

Option B tested repository-separated Python functions, frozen `microsoft/codebert-base` embeddings and three independently predicted AST-derived primitives:

- cyclomatic complexity;
- maximum control nesting depth;
- distinct call-site count.

The preregistered hard-negative result was:

```text
raw cosine:                 0.532458984375
raw Euclidean:              0.533314453125
predicted primitive model:  0.732851562500
best-raw gap:               0.199537109375
outcome:                    REAL_PREMISE_SUPPORTED
```

The complete `4,000 × 5` primary query-score matrix and final decision were independently recomputed by a separate implementation.

The promoted claim remains narrow:

> In repository-separated real Python code, independently predicted AST primitive coordinates exposed the frozen three-way structural relation materially better than raw CodeBERT cosine or Euclidean geometry on the preregistered hard-negative test.

This supports the premise that a real frozen representation can contain useful relational information that its default geometry materially underexposes.

## What RELATE has not established

RELATE has not yet demonstrated:

- a general relational-composition algorithm;
- semantic binding across arbitrary primitive identities;
- calibrated propagation of primitive support;
- safe refusal for unsupported compound queries;
- superiority to a directly trained compound model;
- production-quality nearest-neighbour retrieval;
- transfer across embedding models, programming languages or domains;
- conceptual retrieval in writing.

E01 is closed because its weighted product-space results were valid positive controls but did not establish the original general composition thesis. No later experiment inherits an E01 success claim.

## Why code is the first application

Code contains many objectively measurable relationships beyond textual resemblance:

- control-flow and data-flow structure;
- side-effect and exception behaviour;
- call-graph and dependency roles;
- algorithmic family;
- architectural responsibility;
- duplicated business rules;
- equivalent transformations expressed with different syntax and names.

That makes code a strong scientific proving ground. Ground-truth or independently measured relations can be defined outside the embedding, allowing RELATE to test whether the representation exposes them better through relation-specific coordinates than through one default similarity score.

Option B established one bounded structural premise. Finding conceptual duplicates, architectural equivalents or behaviourally equivalent implementations remains future research.

## Writing as a second application

Writing contains relationships that topic-level semantic similarity often collapses together:

- the same concept expressed through different vocabulary;
- the same causal structure applied to different subjects;
- repeated arguments or contradictions;
- shared narrative function;
- thematic and metaphorical recurrence;
- the evolution of an idea through drafts and author decisions.

This is a proposed application, not an established RELATE result. A writing programme will require author-grounded labels, manuscript lineage, selected and rejected revisions, explicit concept definitions and human evaluation. The repository must not promote writing claims from model-generated annotations alone.

### C1 — confirmation, only if justified

A documentation-only C1 contract may be written only after C0 publishes `C1_CONTRACT_JUSTIFIED`. C1 must then compare the selected propagated-support mechanism against:

- independent primitive conformal abstention;
- a directly trained compound model with its own conformal wrapper;
- relevant confidence and oracle-support diagnostics;
- matched-coverage selective-risk rules fixed before reserve selection.

A C1 failure closes the current propagated-refusal novelty route while preserving Option B. A C1 pass permits one bounded real-domain refusal pilot under a new decision record.

## Repository and conversation boundary

Research conversations are useful source material: they contain intuitions, proposed applications, criticism, rejected paths and unresolved questions. They are not the current scientific state.

```text
conversations = research archive
repository    = research constitution
```

A chat-derived idea enters the repository first as a proposed hypothesis, application or decision question. It becomes a claim only through the claim ledger, a frozen contract, committed evidence, an explicit decision rule and the required verification class.

## Validation status

The historical `tests/` suite is preserved but disabled during the reset. New tests are written under `tests_current/` as stable capability interfaces emerge.

A passing reset-era test suite establishes only that those active interfaces passed. It does not imply that every historical experiment has been behaviourally reverified.
