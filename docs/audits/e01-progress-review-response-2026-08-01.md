# E01 Progress Review Response — 2026-08-01

## Decision

The external progress analysis is accepted in substance.

E01.1 is classified as:

> A successful synthetic composition positive control and harness validation using standard weighted product-space score fusion.

It is not classified as:

- a novel RELATE composition algorithm;
- a general learned-operator composition result;
- a multi-seed replicated finding;
- evidence of support-aware abstention;
- evidence from a real frozen representation.

## Corrections applied to the current record

1. The blog now uses **“What this point estimate showed”** rather than “What we found.”
2. “First nontrivial relational-composition result” is narrowed to **“first non-collapsing synthetic composition result.”**
3. The result is explicitly described as weighted product-space score fusion and a positive-control baseline.
4. The frozen `composition_regret` field is retained but interpreted as oracle triplet disagreement, not regret against a directly trained compound model.
5. The claim ledger now includes a bounded E01.1 replay-verified point-estimate row.
6. The README roadmap and current-status sections now include E01.0 and E01.1.
7. H-005 remains a prospective novelty claim whose required baselines include standard score fusion and directly trained compound comparators.

## Findings accepted without retroactive modification

The following issues are real but belong in E01.2 rather than a rewritten E01.1 executable contract:

- stronger scalar projections;
- every non-identity primitive permutation;
- direct compound metric comparator;
- latent/noise ceiling;
- primitive-level recoverability metrics;
- weak, absent, nonlinear, correlated and shifted primitives;
- explicit hash-addressed triplet manifests;
- Boolean conjunction, exclusion and Pareto retrieval;
- independent metric and decision recomputation.

The seed-307 result, thresholds, decisions and hashes remain frozen.

## Scientific boundary after correction

The strongest supported E01.1 statement is:

> Independently predicted primitive coordinates approximated four preregistered weighted product-space relations in one synthetic seed and exceeded the controls included in the frozen contract.

This supports feasibility of post-hoc composition in a controlled positive case. It does not support algorithmic novelty.

## Gate before implementation of E01.2

E01.2 may proceed only from a preregistered contract that includes:

- fresh seeds excluding `307`;
- strong scalar and directly trained baselines;
- exhaustive wrong alignments;
- explicit triplet manifests;
- primitive-quality reporting and attainable-ceiling analysis;
- unsupported-component conditions;
- non-additive relation families;
- deterministic replay and independent recomputation classifications;
- an all-or-nothing promotion rule.
