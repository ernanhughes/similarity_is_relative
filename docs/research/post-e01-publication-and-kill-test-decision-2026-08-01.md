# Post-E01 Publication and Kill-Test Decision

Date: 2026-08-01

## Decision status

```text
E01 closure: COMPLETE

Option C: CONDITIONAL ON B
Former broad synthetic E02 plan: CANCELLED
```

## What has been decided

The project will publish the E01 audit narrative before beginning another experiment.

The project will not proceed with the former six-stage E02–E07 roadmap or the proposed large synthetic factorisation matrix. That design would measure quantities largely determined by generator settings and would repeat the failure mode identified in E01.

The bounded research path is:

1. finalize and publish the E01 case study;
2. run Option B, one real frozen-representation premise test;
3. run Option C only if Option B passes;
4. stop RELATE if either kill test fails.

## Option B — real-representation premise test

### Question

> Does a real frozen representation contain an objectively measurable compound relation that default cosine and Euclidean geometry expose materially worse than simple supervised primitive readouts?

### Why this comes first

This tests the load-bearing project premise without allowing the experimenter to plant the primitive information or set the difficulty through nuisance dimensions.

A negative result makes synthetic query execution and support propagation irrelevant to the stated motivation.

### Scope

- one frozen pretrained representation;
- one domain;
- a small objective primitive set;
- one preregistered compound query;
- repository-level, project-level or equivalent leakage-resistant splits;
- raw cosine and Euclidean controls;
- primitive-readout executor;
- true-feature oracle;
- triplet accuracy, hard-negative accuracy, recall and neighbour regret.

### Budget

Maximum ten working days from merged contract to final decision.

### Provisional continuation rule

Exact thresholds must be frozen in a separate contract before implementation. The intended shape is:

- material improvement over raw cosine on hard-negative triplet accuracy;
- material improvement over raw cosine on recall@10;
- retention of a large fraction of the true-feature oracle improvement;
- survival under leakage-resistant splits.

Failure of any required condition closes the RELATE research programme.

## Option C — propagated refusal

Option C is not authorized unless Option B passes.

### Question

> Does compound-level propagation of calibrated primitive support improve selective risk at matched coverage beyond independent per-primitive conformal abstention and a directly trained compound model with its own conformal wrapper?

### Scope

- one focused operating regime;
- two imperfect supported primitives;
- one absent primitive;
- one shifted primitive;
- at most three query forms;
- split conformal calibration;
- propagated compound support;
- independent per-primitive conformal baseline;
- direct compound selective baseline;
- uncalibrated confidence baseline;
- risk–coverage curves as the primary evidence.

### Budget

Maximum fifteen working days.

### Kill condition

If propagated support does not beat the strongest prior-art baseline by a preregistered material margin on selective risk at matched coverage, the novelty line closes.

## Prior-art gate

Before Option C runs, the closest applicable prior-art methods must be implemented or represented by faithful baselines. At minimum:

- post-hoc concept bottleneck prediction over a frozen representation;
- independent split-conformal primitive abstention;
- directly trained compound selective prediction;
- relevant unseen-query composition methods where the comparison is technically appropriate.

No novelty language is permitted without an empirical distinction from the closest implemented baseline.

## Methodological deliverable

The evidence-first process is now a separate deliverable from RELATE.

The E01 case establishes at least six reusable rules:

1. attainable-ceiling gate;
2. discriminating-power review;
3. gate-lineage rule;
4. control-validity rule;
5. independent-verification taxonomy;
6. metric-continuity rule.

The project may publish these rules as a methodological case study regardless of the outcome of Options B and C.
