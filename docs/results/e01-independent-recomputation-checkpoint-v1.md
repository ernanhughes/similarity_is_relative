## Decision 1 — E01.1 does not retain its original rule under exhaustive controls

E01.1 required the composed method to exceed every included control by at least `0.10`. Its frozen control set contained one cyclic wrong alignment.

The independent recomputation evaluated all five non-identity permutations. The margins against the strongest permutation were:

| Compound | Exhaustive margin | Original `0.10` rule |
|---|---:|---|
| `a2_b` | `0.0853` | fail |
| `a3_c` | `0.1464` | pass |
| `b2_c` | `0.0788` | fail |
| `a_b2_c3` | `0.0374` | fail |

Audit decision:

```text
ORIGINAL_RULE_FAILS_WITH_EXHAUSTIVE_CONTROL
```

This does not alter the frozen E01.1 artifact. It changes its interpretation: the original four-of-four point-estimate result was conditional on the weaker frozen control set.

## Retrieval context

The headline triplet accuracies were around `0.92–0.93`, while mean recall@10 was materially lower:

| Compound | Mean recall@10 |
|---|---:|
| `a2_b` | `0.1723` |
| `a3_c` | `0.1614` |
| `b2_c` | `0.1697` |
| `a_b2_c3` | `0.3542` |

The latent oracle produced effectively the same retrieval profile. The gap is therefore a property of the noisy ranking target and evaluation geometry, not a separate learned-model defect.

## Final classification

E01.1 and E01.2a remain:

- numerically reproducible;
- useful as implementation and positive-control records;
- evidence that preserving predicted coordinates avoids the registered scalar collapses.

They do not establish:

- general relational composition;
- semantic relation-name identity;
- a novel RELATE algorithm;
- support propagation;
- calibrated refusal;
- real-representation transfer.

The weighted-product-space E01 research line is closed. Future research begins from the redesigned support-aware relational query-runtime thesis and must use a non-saturated generator, fair direct-compound baselines, explicit query algebra, hard negatives, ceiling measurements and unsupported-query controls.
